Graduation parties bring together relatives, classmates, neighbors, and family friends. Everyone sees a slightly different version of the day.

That makes guest photos valuable. They capture reactions, decorations, food, speeches, and casual groups the graduate may miss.

## Shared albums

Shared albums are a good option when the guest list is mostly close family. They are easy to revisit and keep photos in one place.

They are less reliable when the party includes many classmates or family friends who do not want to join another album.

## Social posts

Social posts are great for celebrating the graduate publicly. They are not a complete collection method.

Some guests will not post, some accounts are private, and stories can disappear before anyone saves them.

## QR code gallery

A QR code gallery is simple for mixed guest lists. Put the code near the card box, dessert table, or entrance, and guests can upload directly from their phones.

It also makes the gallery easy to share with relatives who could not attend.

## Quick recommendation

For a graduation party, use a QR code gallery as the main collection point and encourage social posts separately. That keeps private guest photos organized while still letting people celebrate publicly.

## FAQ

### How do I collect graduation party photos from guests?

Put a QR code near the entrance, card box, dessert table, or display table so guests can add photos from their phones.

### Should we use social media for graduation photos?

Social posts are great for public celebration, but a private gallery is better for collecting photos from everyone in one place.

### Can relatives who missed the party see the gallery?

Yes. After the event, share the gallery link with relatives and friends who could not attend.
