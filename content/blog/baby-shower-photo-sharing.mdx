At a baby shower, the host is usually busy keeping the event moving. The guest of honor is busy talking to everyone. That means many of the best photos are taken by guests.

The easier the upload process is, the more complete the gallery will be.

## Group texts are easy but scattered

A group text can work for a small shower. It is quick and casual.

For larger showers, it becomes messy. Guests may send duplicate photos, message threads become noisy, and the host has to manually save everything.

## Shared albums are useful after the shower

Shared albums are more organized than a text thread. They are especially useful when close friends and family already use the same photo app.

The barrier is joining. If guests need to accept an invite or use a specific account, some photos will never make it in.

## QR code cards fit naturally into the decor

Baby showers already use printed signs, table cards, and games. A small QR code card can fit next to the guest book, gift table, or dessert table.

The instruction can be simple: scan to add your photos to the shower gallery.

## Quick recommendation

For a small shower, a shared album may be fine. For a larger shower with mixed guests, a QR code gallery is easier because guests can upload without downloading an app or waiting for an invitation.

## FAQ

### How do I collect baby shower photos from guests?

Use one QR code gallery and place the code near the guest book, gift table, dessert table, or activity area.

### Is a shared album enough for a baby shower?

It can be enough for a small shower with close family. For mixed guest lists, a browser-based QR code upload page has less friction.

### Should guests upload videos too?

Yes, if your gallery supports them. Short videos of games, speeches, and gift reactions often become some of the best memories.
